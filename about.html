About BrightPath Digital Academy

BrightPath Digital Academy is a modern technology learning center dedicated to equipping young professionals with practical ICT skills.

Founded in Kampala, Uganda, we bridge the digital skills gap by offering industry-aligned, hands-on computer courses taught by expert coaches.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, practical, and high-quality digital skills training that enables learners to excel in technological careers.

Our Vision

To become a premier regional digital innovation hub producing skilled technology practitioners across East Africa.
